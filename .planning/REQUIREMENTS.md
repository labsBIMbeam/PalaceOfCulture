# Requirements: 600 Billion — The Palace of Culture

**Defined:** 2026-07-26
**Core Value:** A builder can enter Werkstattgasse, create and place something meaningful in under ten minutes, and invite another person to witness or remix it.

## v1 Requirements

Requirements for the initial staged release. Each requirement is a one-to-one, user-centric conversion of one approved extracted requirement and maps to exactly one roadmap phase.

### Ethical Time & Homecoming

- [ ] **RHYT-01**: A player can anticipate visible, deterministic future outcomes while absence only compounds progress and never causes decay, missed-play punishment, manipulative retention prompts, or paid acceleration.
- [ ] **RHYT-02**: A player can experience minute homecoming, daily tending, weekly Bell, monthly age and Moon Night, and year-plus timelock-maturity cadences while missing the sole weekly appointment loses no material progress and its echo remains available for 48 hours.
- [ ] **RHYT-03**: A Home owner can see placed production buildings age through permanent patina and capped specialty tiers from wall-clock time, with offline accrual honored and age reset only when a building is absorbed.
- [ ] **RHYT-04**: A returning player can dismiss a one-click Hearth Report of at most five already-completed crafts, arrivals, age changes, and offline catch-up outcomes without claiming or unlocking progress.
- [ ] **RHYT-05**: A Home owner can attract up to five inhabitants through visible, deterministic sustain conditions tied to placed objects and building age, with catch-up arrivals and a total production bonus capped at 10%.
- [ ] **RHYT-06**: A community member can discover a NIP-52 Sunday Bell and deterministic block-height Market Day, Moon Night, and Halving festivals whose recurring experiences and cosmetics never alter material progression.
- [ ] **RHYT-07**: A visitor can preserve a visit with one signed postcard, and an eligible long-horizon completion can produce an echoed Unsealing with up to 21 permanent nearby witness records and no gameplay buff.
- [ ] **RHYT-08**: A guild member can contribute signed material gifts to one wall-time monument at a time, receive contributions back on cancellation, and see chronological—not amount-ranked—attribution without pooled spendable custody, quotas, or acceleration.

### Private Home Progression

- [ ] **HOME-01**: A Home owner can grow an empty plot into a production town where resource drip, placed production buildings, offline craft queues, and inhabitants progress through elapsed time rather than gathering, grinding, or purchases.
- [ ] **HOME-02**: A Home owner can extend the same server-compatible blueprint in the sequence production buildings → age-based building lines → inhabitants → read-only visits, without turning Home into the public build surface.

### Palace Prism & Sessions

- [ ] **PRSM-01**: A member can use one Palace Core across accessible web, world, and sandboxed game views while guild lenses, canonical IDs, Session continuity, honest quiet states, and untrusted-game boundaries remain consistent at 1280×720 and with 3D disabled.
- [ ] **PRSM-02**: A member can navigate a persistent Palace Prism with guild rail and context, switchable center renderer, real People and Now, and Session dock without remounting active Session or chat.
- [ ] **PRSM-03**: A member can enter a Guild Quarter depth view that resolves the same persistent guild objects in web and world, while routine actions remain available outside the Quarter and small guilds need no fabricated architecture.
- [ ] **PRSM-04**: A participant can join a Session that transforms Prism into Bell Mode with fixed leave, invite, web, world, and play controls, then leave and recover the prior guild lens and scroll position.
- [ ] **PRSM-05**: A participant can follow or directly join any step of an Arcane Night program whose Wavlake warm-up, guild gathering, Arcane challenge, and aftershow remain separate canonical Activities and Sessions.
- [ ] **PRSM-06**: A participant can move between web and world while one persistent PalaceShell preserves chat, playback, Session, participants, and serializable location state, with authorization before room admission.
- [ ] **PRSM-07**: A participant can use a validated prototype where one Activity appears through two lenses without duplication, Session/chat survive two web↔world cycles, Commons works with zero humans and no 3D, and one sandboxed Arcane Game receives no Palace authority before general guild creation begins.

### Social Art

- [ ] **ART-01**: A player can turn intentional waiting or walking into a small encounter, attentive dialogue into a memory fragment, and memory plus inspiration into an artifact placed as a shared community trace without an algorithmic feed becoming the primary loop.
- [ ] **ART-02**: A builder can complete one local/mock walkable Werkstattgasse slice from noisy-feed refusal through presence, encounter, memory, artifact creation, finite-slot placement, visible world change, and an inviteable witness/remix handoff without requiring real social-network feeds, generative AI, a full economy, broad persistence, multiplayer scale, or a large quest system.

### Communal Crafting

- [ ] **CRFT-01**: A contributor can make a durable social crafting promise through repair, real process time, complementary roles, attributed consent-based contributions, maintenance rituals, and finite sufficiency without paid skips, coercive idling, captive labor, or combat dependency.
- [ ] **CRFT-02**: A contributor can fill material, craft, care, design, teaching, or funding slots on a public work order, while commissioning requires the physical bundle, all transformations, two complementary contribution kinds, authentic first use, and shared acknowledgement rather than money alone.
- [ ] **CRFT-03**: A participant can complete a legible Locktard Street bakery chain one obvious action at a time from material contribution through visible transformation to permanent hospitality capability, with the street and Ledger changed and the demonstration repeatably reset at stable laptop frame rate.

### Earned Palace Placement

- [ ] **PLAC-01**: A builder can convert private milestones into earned furnishing rights by selecting an unlocked object, previewing its footprint, placing it only in an allowed finite Palace slot, inspecting the result, and seeing richer objects at higher capability tiers.
- [ ] **PLAC-02**: A curator or builder can distinguish furniture/deco, culture, special, teaching, hero, timelock, and later structural asset classes, with sources and stronger controls where appropriate and structural parts deferred until furnishing is fun.
- [ ] **PLAC-03**: A builder can receive a timelock asset only from valid non-custodial proof—Bitcoin L1 by default and for legend tiers, Liquid only as constrained fallback, never Lightning, normal resources, scores, trust, or currency—while principal remains server-independent.
- [ ] **PLAC-04**: A builder can preview and place rectangular footprints on the 0.7m furnishing grid only where finite personal, room, guild, exhibit, stage, or monument slot rules allow, and receives visible rejection for invalid slot, tier, trust, count, approval, or fit conditions.
- [ ] **PLAC-05**: A builder can enter the social world, see finite slots, select an honestly mocked unlocked object, preview and place it legally, inspect the visible result, and observe higher-tier unlocks before terraforming, paid placement, open upload, spam-scale persistence, or structural construction exists.

## v2 Requirements

No additional extracted requirements were assigned to v2. Deferred spatial and platform concepts are recorded under Out of Scope and require an explicit future milestone decision before promotion.

## Out of Scope

| Feature | Reason |
|---------|--------|
| Mobile gameplay in `apps/web` | Locked ADR-0007 makes mobile web companion-only; future gameplay is a separate app. |
| Palace HQ interior, HQ Arrival Plaza, and Palace Ringstadt as current runtime | The current playable and first public realtime target is linear Werkstattgasse; these are future contexts. |
| Full private-world builder, terraforming, and structural construction | The staged social placement loop and furnishing must ship first. |
| Open uploads, paid placement, infinite/spam placement | Finite curated slots and earned objects preserve legibility and curation. |
| Punitive retention, decay, streaks, login rewards, pay-to-skip, or wealth/activity ranking | These contradict the ethical anticipation and low-time-preference design laws. |
| Spendable pooled guild treasury, tithes, quotas, and amount-ranked plaques | Guild economics remain direct-recipient or materials-only and non-custodial. |
| Lightning-backed timelock assets | The approved proof model uses Bitcoin L1, with only constrained Liquid fallback. |
| General guild creator before interface prototype gates | Prism continuity, no-3D/quiet states, and one sandboxed Arcane Game must validate first. |
