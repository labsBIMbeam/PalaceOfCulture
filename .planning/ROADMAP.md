# Roadmap: 600 Billion — The Palace of Culture

## Overview

The roadmap starts with the smallest complete expression of the Core Value in the current playable district: a builder enters Werkstattgasse, turns attentive presence into an artifact, places it visibly, and hands it to another person to witness or remix. It then makes placement earned and proof-bound, deepens co-creation through communal crafting, gives private Home progression an ethical wall-clock rhythm, connects that rhythm to shared calendars and permanent cultural traces, and finally unifies those activities through one persistent Palace Prism and Session model. Each phase is a vertical user-visible capability; durable truth, non-custodial proofs, and deterministic auditability remain cross-cutting invariants rather than horizontal implementation phases.

## Phases

**Phase Numbering:** Sequential integer phases are planned milestone work; decimal phases are reserved for urgent later insertions.

- [ ] **Phase 1: Visible Art in Werkstattgasse** - Deliver the under-ten-minute enter → create → place → invite → witness/remix vertical slice.
- [ ] **Phase 2: Earned and Proof-Bound Palace Objects** - Make finite placement rights, asset classes, and non-custodial timelock objects legible and enforceable.
- [ ] **Phase 3: Communal Crafting and Commissioning** - Turn shared work into durable, attributed capability through one complete bakery chain.
- [ ] **Phase 4: Homecoming and Ethical Time** - Grow Home through elapsed-time production, age, inhabitants, reports, and non-punitive cadence.
- [ ] **Phase 5: Shared Calendar and Cultural Memory** - Make gatherings, visits, maturities, and guild monuments visible without scarcity or pooled custody.
- [ ] **Phase 6: Palace Prism and Live Sessions** - Unify guild lenses, renderer continuity, Sessions, Bell Mode, and Arcane Night in one persistent shell.

## Phase Details

### Phase 1: Visible Art in Werkstattgasse

**Goal**: A builder can enter Werkstattgasse, create and place something meaningful in under ten minutes, and invite another person to witness or remix the visible result.
**Depends on**: Nothing (first phase)
**Requirements**: ART-01, ART-02, PLAC-05
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):

  1. A desktop-browser builder can enter Werkstattgasse and complete the path from intentional presence to a placed, inspectable artifact in under ten minutes.
  2. Waiting or walking can produce one encounter, attentive dialogue can produce one memory, and memory plus inspiration can produce an artifact whose placement visibly changes the world.
  3. The builder can see finite slots, select an honestly mocked unlocked object, preview its footprint, receive visible rejection for an invalid fit, place it legally, and inspect the result.
  4. The builder can invite another person into a bounded handoff where the placed artifact can be witnessed or remixed, without requiring a full social feed, broad persistence, generative AI, or multiplayer scale.

### Phase 2: Earned and Proof-Bound Palace Objects

**Goal**: Builders can express earned private progress through curated Palace objects whose finite placement, provenance, and proof rules are visible and trustworthy.
**Depends on**: Phase 1
**Requirements**: PLAC-01, PLAC-02, PLAC-03, PLAC-04
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):

  1. A builder can select an earned furnishing, preview its rectangular footprint on the 0.7m grid, place it only in an allowed finite slot, and inspect the result and next capability tier.
  2. Invalid slot, footprint, tier, trust, count, or curator-approval conditions are rejected visibly before placement changes Palace state.
  3. Builders and curators can distinguish decorative, culture, special, teaching, hero, and timelock assets and see class-appropriate sources and controls, while structural parts remain unavailable.
  4. A timelock asset appears only after valid non-custodial proof; Bitcoin L1 is the default and legend-tier path, constrained Liquid fallback is explicit, Lightning is rejected, and principal remains recoverable without Palace custody.

### Phase 3: Communal Crafting and Commissioning

**Goal**: Contributors can turn complementary work and real process time into a lasting, visibly attributed public capability.
**Depends on**: Phase 2
**Requirements**: CRFT-01, CRFT-02, CRFT-03
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):

  1. A contributor can choose a material, craft, care, design, teaching, or funding role on a public work order and see each contribution attributed without a single ranked progress bar.
  2. Commissioning remains unavailable until the physical bundle, required transformations, two complementary contribution kinds, authentic first use, and shared acknowledgement are all present.
  3. Participants can complete the Locktard Street bakery chain one obvious action at a time and see both the street and Ledger gain a permanent hospitality capability.
  4. The demonstration resets repeatably at stable laptop frame rate and offers no paid skip, coercive idle mechanic, inventory spreadsheet, or combat dependency.

### Phase 4: Homecoming and Ethical Time

**Goal**: Home owners can make durable progress through understandable elapsed-time systems that reward return without punishing absence.
**Depends on**: Phase 3
**Requirements**: RHYT-01, RHYT-02, RHYT-03, RHYT-04, RHYT-05, HOME-01, HOME-02
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):

  1. A Home owner can grow an empty plot through resource drip, placed production buildings, offline craft queues, and inhabitants while wall-clock time—not grinding, attendance, or purchases—remains the bottleneck.
  2. Returning after an absence applies deterministic catch-up automatically and shows a dismissible Hearth Report of at most five completed outcomes without claim gates, expiry, decay, or lost progress.
  3. Placed production buildings visibly age through permanent patina and capped multipliers, honor offline accrual, and reset age only when absorbed.
  4. Visible sustain conditions attract up to five inhabitants with catch-up support and a total production bonus capped at 10%, independent of popularity or payment.
  5. The minute, daily, weekly, monthly, and year-plus cadence is legible, missed appointments cost no material progress, and Home advances in the documented building → age → inhabitant → read-only visit sequence.

### Phase 5: Shared Calendar and Cultural Memory

**Goal**: Community time, visits, long-horizon completions, and guild work leave durable cultural traces without material coercion or pooled spendable custody.
**Depends on**: Phase 4
**Requirements**: RHYT-06, RHYT-07, RHYT-08
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):

  1. A participant can discover the weekly Sunday Bell and deterministic block-height Market Day, Moon Night, and Halving events through NIP-52 calendar data, including their bounded duration and echoes.
  2. Calendar gatherings yield only recurring social or cosmetic experiences and never change resource drip, timelock maturity, material progression, or missed-play outcomes.
  3. A visitor can leave one signed postcard per visit, browse 21 displayed cards with archived overflow, and witness an eligible echoed Unsealing whose permanent witness list is capped at 21 and grants no buff.
  4. A guild can build one wall-time monument from signed material gifts, cancel with contributions returned, and display chronological attribution without a sats treasury, tithe, quota, headcount gate, amount ranking, or activity acceleration.

### Phase 6: Palace Prism and Live Sessions

**Goal**: Members can move among guild lenses, web and world renderers, and sandboxed activities through one persistent Palace Core and Session experience.
**Depends on**: Phase 5
**Requirements**: PRSM-01, PRSM-02, PRSM-03, PRSM-04, PRSM-05, PRSM-06, PRSM-07
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):

  1. A member can switch guild, Joined, and Commons lenses and web/world center renderers while canonical Activities, guild curations, People and Now, Session, chat, media, and participants retain identity and continuity.
  2. The shell remains functional and honest at 1280×720, with 3D disabled, with an empty world, and with Commons at zero humans; switching views does not fabricate architecture or population.
  3. A member can enter a Guild Quarter through either web or world using the same persistent object IDs, while routine actions remain available outside the Quarter.
  4. Joining a Session activates Bell Mode with fixed leave, invite, web, world, and play controls; leaving restores the previous lens and scroll position, and two web↔world cycles preserve Session and chat.
  5. Participants can join any step of the canonical Arcane Night program, while the sandboxed Arcane Game receives no Palace authority and the general guild creator remains gated until all prototype checks pass.
