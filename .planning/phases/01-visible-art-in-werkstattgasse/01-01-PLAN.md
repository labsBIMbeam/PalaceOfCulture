## ASVS-L1 scope

This is an ASVS-L1-style client boundary checklist, not a certification claim. Critical/high threats block completion until their listed automated mitigation is green.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| World/DOM intent → application reducer | Keyboard, pointer, proximity, and overlay events are untrusted intent; only the pure reducer may authorize a Phase-1 transition. |
| Application reducer → scene/UI effects | Accepted application state crosses into visual, accessible, and lifecycle effects; effects cannot write truth back. |
| Application accepted delta → transport adapter | The adapter is allowed to open only after acceptance and exposes no SDK object or truth-setting callback to scene/UI. |
| Legacy mock/multiplayer inputs → Phase-1 proof path | Ambient townsfolk, backlog, loopback, handles, participant counts, and remote presence are untrusted and categorically ineligible evidence. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Specific mitigation |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|---------------------|
| T-01-01 | Spoofing | Phase-1 social/activation evidence | high | mitigate | Keep all fake/ambient/backlog/loopback/count/Kerni/timer/presentation inputs outside `Phase1RelayAction` acceptance authority; focused smoke asserts they cannot reach accepted or `OPEN`. |
| T-01-02 | Tampering | `reducePhase1Relay` transition order | high | mitigate | Require inactive → matching pending attempt → matching accepted action; stale, duplicate, forged, cancelled, failed, and out-of-order actions preserve prior state in task-level TDD. |
| T-01-03 | Repudiation | Activation attempt correlation | medium | mitigate | Carry a phase-owned attempt identifier through pending and terminal actions and assert mismatched attempts are no-ops, producing deterministic replayable transition evidence in the smoke. |
| T-01-04 | Information disclosure | Phase1Relay transport/UI contract | low | accept | The tracer carries no message content, key, signer, URL, handle, participant identity, or external event; retain this data-minimized interface and add no logging of user data. |
| T-01-05 | Denial of service | Premature/repeated transport lifecycle | medium | mitigate | Keep lifecycle inert before accepted state, execute only on a new accepted delta, make repeated accepted renders idempotent, and dispose on scene teardown. |
| T-01-06 | Elevation of privilege | Scene/UI/transport effect → application truth | high | mitigate | `PalaceScene`, `Phase1RelayOverlay`, and the adapter may dispatch intent or present state but cannot assign accepted state; source contracts require reducer-before-effect wiring and forbid SDK imports in scene/UI. |
| T-01-07 | Tampering | Pending/failure presentation | high | mitigate | Render `OPEN` only from accepted reducer state; server-render assertions prove inactive, pending, failed, and cancelled states expose no accepted text or semantics. |
</threat_model>
