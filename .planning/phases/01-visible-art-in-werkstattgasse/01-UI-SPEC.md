# Phase 1 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction source of truth for the desktop-Web Werkstattgasse vertical slice. Generated by `gsd-ui-researcher`; approval remains with `gsd-ui-checker`.

---

## Experience Intent

The phase must feel like leaving a noisy layer and noticing a handmade place. It is dark cyberpunk without militarism: warm soot, copper, timber, framed windows, small street machines, mushrooms, workshop clutter, and natural idle poses. It must not drift into white-fantasy architecture, tactical HUD language, loot presentation, quest authority, or a generic social dashboard.

The emotional sequence is fixed:

`raccoon → Kerni → Street glimpse → fictional feed → put away → presence → explicit help → three physical parts → one lit relay → voluntary invite → real signed pulse → optional additive lens`

The player begins genuinely alone with small, peripheral Kerni. The relay's accepted light changes the world from isolated to capable of communication. A second person's contribution is visible only after explicit human signing; silence remains honest.

### Non-negotiable visual laws

- World first: DOM overlays never cover more than 34% of the 1280×720 viewport except the intro, fictional feed intrusion, and signer consent sheet.
- Warm workshop, not command center: avoid crosshair-led objectives, mission copy, ranks, medals, countdowns, progress bars, minimaps, and persistent NPC nameplates.
- Kerni is a Schaffenwerkzeug and companion, rendered at the existing small scale beside the Z1 bench; never center-framed as an authority.
- Handmade relay forms use dark ceramic, hammered copper, smoked glass, visible fasteners, and a restrained amber core. No pristine white marble, chrome sci-fi console, weapon silhouette, or loot rarity glow.
- Street composition preserves a clear center walk lane, asymmetric self-modeled architecture, framed windows, mushrooms/machines, small props, and believable resting/working poses.
- Light, animation, sound, labels, and Kerni reactions only present accepted application state; they never create it.

## Typography

Exactly four phase sizes and two weights are allowed. Existing font-family tokens are reused; phase surfaces normalize the many legacy sizes to this set.

| Role | Size | Weight | Line Height | Usage |
|------|------|--------|-------------|-------|
| Utility | 12px | 700 | 1.35 | keycaps, status, kickers, attribution |
| Body | 14px | 400 | 1.55 | dialogue, feed cards, explanations, errors |
| Heading | 20px | 700 | 1.25 | cards, consent sheet, relay panel |
| Display | 28px | 700 | 1.15 | intro/story title only |

- Uppercase is limited to short utility labels (`OPEN`, `E`, agent label, status kicker), with `0.08em` tracking.
- Body and action labels use sentence case. Never set dialogue, error text, or the Kerni line in all caps.
- Long body copy wraps; controls may wrap to two lines and grow vertically. No essential copy is ellipsized.

## Surface and Interaction Contracts

### 1. Canonical intro: video → cards → `Walk in`

- Intro is a full-viewport dark surface with a centered maximum-520px story card, using the existing pre-engine soot panel and gold border language.
- Play `/intro.mp4` once as the approved five-second raccoon-to-Kerni visual. It is decorative: failure, mute, or reduced effects goes directly to story card 1 without losing canon.
- A video skip action may only move to story card 1. The three authoritative cards remain in order, one per deliberate advance; `Walk in` appears only on card 3.
- Do not add pagination numbers, elapsed time, or autoplay countdown. A three-mark motif may indicate card count, but only the current mark changes and it has an accessible label such as `Story 2 of 3`.
- Preserve the locally canonical card copy from `onboardingStory.ts` verbatim:
  1. `CHOMP` — `A raccoon bites your finger. One tiny drop of blood.` — `“I can't see blood.” You immediately faint.`
  2. `DIAGNOSTIC` — `You wake in a pile of copper parts. The raccoon is now Kerni.` — `“Builder offline. Cause: three millimetres of blood.” Suggestion only.`
  3. `LOCKTARD STREET` — `Thirty-six sockets. No owner. One unfinished spaceship.` — `“We're not a cult. We're culture.” Build one small part. Place it. Invite someone.`
- `Walk in` is the only final action. It receives initial focus on card 3.

### 2. Street glimpse and fictional feed intrusion

- After `Walk in`, show the Street unobstructed for 2.4 seconds. Camera control is available immediately; do not freeze movement for the glimpse.
- The fictional feed then enters from the right over 240ms and occupies `min(384px, 34vw)`; at 1280×720 it shows all three cards and the action without required scroll.
- Feed header: `THE NEARBY WIRE` with a `FICTIONAL` badge. No avatars, reaction counts, likes, reposts, view counts, compose input, or current headlines.
- Exact cards:
  - `WORKSHOP NOTE` — `The mushroom sorter has rejected one perfectly ordinary spoon.`
  - `STREET WEATHER` — `Copper dust after dusk. The framed windows remain opinionated.`
  - `LOCAL STATUS` — `A small machine is still humming beside a bench nobody reserved.`
- One large full-width `Put away` action is pinned below the cards. It is always available and requires no scroll, reaction, or read acknowledgement.
- `Escape` activates the same dismissal when focus is inside the feed. Dismissal restores scene focus without simulating a click in the world.
- The feed may be reopened from a low-priority `Wire` button, but doing so pauses attention accumulation only; it cannot remove already accepted presence.

### 3. Presence choreography, 36 seconds

Presence is a quiet environmental transition, not a task. No UI may show a timer, number, objective, checkpoint list, progress meter, quest marker, or percentage.

| Internal interval | Full-effects presentation | Muted / reduced-effects / subtitle presentation |
|-------------------|---------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| 0–8s attentive | Street mix loses feed-like high-frequency hiss; nearby machine sound separates from ambience | Static subtitle: `[The wire falls quiet. Workshop sounds come forward.]` for 3s; machine vane turns to a clear resting angle |
| 8–20s attentive | Z1 framed windows warm by a maximum 12%; one mushroom cap and hanging tool react subtly to passing air | Subtitle: `[Warm light settles in the workshop windows.]`; framed window mullions gain a visible amber edge, no bloom required |
| 20–30s attentive | One street machine slows into an uneven handmade rhythm; two small props settle; Kerni's amber lens catches light once | Subtitle: `[A small machine finds a slower rhythm.]`; machine indicator changes from three short ticks to one steady notch |
| 30–36s attentive | Bench lamp and fixed socket gain a restrained warm rim; Kerni tilts toward the bench without moving toward the player | Subtitle: `[The workbench is easier to notice now.]`; socket gains a persistent etched ring and `WORKBENCH` proximity cue |
| accepted presence | Environment remains in the settled state | Persistent non-animated settled cues remain; no celebration toast |

“Attentive” means the feed is away and the player is either walking or quietly present in the world. Feed reopening pauses further accumulation and never decrements or resets accepted time. Tab suspension does not silently complete the choreography. The accepted `present` truth is monotonic for the phase run.

Audio has a visible `Muted` toggle and subtitles default to on when audio is muted. Reduced effects disables bloom, camera impulse, pulsing, particle drift, and brightness ramps while preserving static edge, text, and shape changes.

### 4. Kerni proximity and one-line orientation

- Kerni is always present beside the first Z1 workbench at the existing small familiar scale. Presence only makes the bench/Kerni easier to notice; it does not spawn or unlock Kerni.
- At 2.6m proximity, show a bottom-center 44px-minimum interaction control: keycap `E`, action `Listen to Kerni`, and a small secondary inspect label `KERNI · WORLD AGENT · SUGGESTION ONLY`.
- The label is visible only in proximity or inspect state, never as a permanent floating nameplate.
- `E` or click speaks exactly once:
  - `Welcome. No rush — the Palace gets better when people leave something useful behind. Start with one small thing.`
- Render the line in a centered soot dialogue panel with subtitle semantics. It remains dismissible with `Escape`, `E`, or `Close Kerni dialogue`; auto-dismiss after 12 seconds only if focus is not inside the panel.
- Kerni may tilt/gesture toward the bench but never blocks the path, follows, selects, assembles, places, signs, publishes, or changes application truth.
- After accepted relay placement, Kerni performs one nonverbal acknowledgement: a 10° lens tilt and one 600ms copper-ring settle. Reduced motion uses a static lens-brightness change for 1.2s. There is no spoken line, quest completion, applause, or authority badge.

### 5. Three-part physical relay assembly and one Z1 socket

The workbench holds exactly three visible parts, each with a distinct silhouette and material:

1. **Copper foot** — low hammered-copper base with three visible fasteners.
2. **Ceramic coil** — short obsidian ceramic cylinder wrapped in a coarse copper loop.
3. **Amber aperture** — small framed smoked-glass lens with an amber center.

Interaction is physical and keyboard-compatible: approach the highlighted part, press `E` to pick it up, aim/move, then press `E` to set it into the matching bench cradle. The same actions are available as a 44px on-screen control. No text field, AI generation, inventory grid, recipe menu, generalized crafting, drag-only gesture, or Kerni automation is permitted.

- Assembly order is foot → coil → aperture, one obvious part at a time.
- Completed assembly becomes one carried relay. The only legal destination is the brass-collared socket fixed to the Z1 bench edge.
- The fixed socket is visible before assembly as an empty physical collar; it is not an objective beacon.
- Fit feedback:
  - `VALID`: teal etched outline + check notch + `E · Place relay`.
  - `INVALID`: coral crossed seam + short lateral physical resistance + `This relay fits the workbench socket.` The player keeps holding the relay.
  - `PENDING`: relay sits unlit in the collar; control reads `Securing relay…`; no invite, `OPEN`, pulse, or Kerni reaction appears.
  - `ACCEPTED`: relay snaps the final 4cm, amber aperture lights, `OPEN` becomes available, and Kerni gives the nonverbal response.
- Invalid or failed placement never leaves a persistent light. Pending lasting longer than 8 seconds becomes failure: `The relay did not secure. Try the socket again.` with `Try again` and `Keep holding` actions.
- The accepted relay light is a small amber core, a 1.4m warm pool, and an etched-ring edge; it must remain readable with bloom off and within the mobile 30 FPS rendering budget.

### 6. `OPEN`, voluntary invite, and sharing fallbacks

After accepted activation, a restrained diegetic plate beside the relay reads `OPEN` with an open-circle icon. It does not show participant counts, “live” urgency, popularity, or a flashing badge.

- Proximity action: `E · Inspect relay`.
- Inspect panel heading: `Relay is OPEN`.
- Body: `The light is on. Invite one person when you want to.`
- Primary: `Copy invite`.
- Secondary: `Not now`.
- Copy pending: `Copying invite…`; control disabled only during the clipboard attempt.
- Copy success: `Invite copied` plus selectable sanitized URL and `Close invite`.
- Manual fallback: heading `Copy the invite manually`; selectable URL; actions `I copied it` and `Close invite`.
- Cancellation closes the panel, returns focus to the relay, and leaves sharing incomplete and the relay `OPEN`.
- Failure: `The invite could not be copied. Select the link or try again.` with `Try again` and `Close invite`.
- The invite is never auto-published, broadcast, or counted as shared merely because the panel or fallback link was shown.

### 7. Invited landing, consent, signed pulse, and replay

A sanitized `?join=street` invite bypasses creator intro and feed. The invited person lands in the same Street with the camera framed 3–5m from the lit relay, with free movement immediately available.

- Initial label: `INVITED TO A LIT RELAY`; body: `Touch the relay to answer. Nothing is sent until you approve a signature.`
- Proximity action: `E · Touch relay`. Mere arrival, proximity, room connection, handle, or participant count does not witness.
- Touch opens a focused consent sheet:
  - Kicker: `SIGNED LIGHT PULSE`
  - Heading: `Send one light pulse?`
  - Body: `Your signer will approve one bounded witness pulse for this relay. This does not post a message or give the Palace control of your key.`
  - Primary: `Sign pulse`
  - Secondary: `Not now`
- Pending: `Waiting for your signer…` with a non-spinning three-notch indicator and active `Cancel signing`.
- Success: `Pulse accepted` and `Your signed light reached this relay.` Only accepted application evidence triggers one 900ms cyan path through the amber relay, then both colors settle.
- Signer cancellation: `No pulse was sent. The relay stays OPEN.` with `Close pulse status` and `Try again`.
- Failure/invalid evidence: `The pulse could not be verified. Nothing changed.` with `Try again` and `Close pulse status`.
- Replay/duplicate or reconnect baseline: no light animation and no completion toast. Show `Already witnessed · no new pulse sent` only when the player explicitly touches again.
- Focus returns to the relay after every terminal state. `Escape`/`Not now` never sends or signs.

### 8. One additive signal lens

After an accepted pulse, the invited person receives one optional proximity action: `E · Add a signal lens`.

- The lens is a small cyan smoked-glass disc in a copper clip, visibly secondary to the amber aperture.
- Preview shows a single valid clip point on the relay's side. The original relay does not recolor, resize, disappear, or lose its plate.
- Consent copy: `Attach one small lens? The original relay and its maker remain visible.` Actions: `Attach lens` and `Leave it as it is`.
- Pending uses `Attaching lens…`; failure uses `The lens was not accepted. The relay is unchanged.` with recovery actions `Try attaching again` and `Leave lens off`.
- Accepted lens settles with a 420ms mechanical click and a static cyan edge. Reduced motion uses the final state immediately plus subtitle `[A second signal lens clicks into place.]`.
- Inspect attribution remains chronological and non-ranked:
  - `RELAY · BUILT BY {creator attribution}`
  - `SIGNAL LENS · ADDED BY {invited signer attribution}`
- Show only attribution actually supported by accepted application evidence. A session handle is not presented as verified human identity.

### 9. Honest no-answer / empty state

The empty social state is a valid, lasting outcome:

- Heading: `Relay is OPEN`.
- Body: `No answer yet. The light stays on.`
- Actions: `Copy invite` and `Close relay`.
- The amber light remains steady. There is no decay, penalty, retry pressure, urgency copy, sadness animation, fake avatar, fake townsfolk, ambient chat, ghost session, fabricated pulse, “people nearby,” social-proof number, or Kerni intervention.
- Existing mock townsfolk, scheduled messages, room backlog, broad `ChatPanel`, remote-player labels, and multiplayer online count are hidden/gated from this Phase 1 path unless backed by the explicitly invited, accepted handoff. None can satisfy witness or remix.

## Application Truth vs Presentation State

Presentation must derive from application-owned facts. It must never infer completion from animation end, elapsed UI time, DOM visibility, transport callbacks, participant count, or Kerni behavior.

| Application truth | Allowed presentation | Forbidden inference |
|-------------------|----------------------|---------------------|
| `introBeat` accepted | video/card shown; `Walk in` available only at final beat | video ended means cards were seen |
| `feedState` plus monotonic attentive duration | glimpse/feed/presence choreography | panel dismissal alone grants presence |
| `presenceAccepted` | settled light/sound/props and bench cue | brightness or subtitle completion sets truth |
| `kerniLineAcknowledged` | one dialogue and later quiet state | Kerni dialogue unlocks/authorizes crafting |
| accepted part-placement facts (0–3) | corresponding physical parts in cradles | local mesh position advances assembly |
| placement validation result | valid/invalid/pending/accepted socket treatment | snap animation or light means accepted |
| `relayOpen` | persistent amber light, `OPEN`, invite eligibility | transport connection or relay callback opens it |
| invite share state | copying/copied/manual/cancel/failure copy | merely showing a URL counts as sharing |
| validated signed witness | one cyan pulse and witness attribution | presence, signer prompt, mock event, or callback counts |
| accepted additive lens | one cyan lens and second attribution row | preview or local attachment counts as remix |

Required phase state vocabulary:

- `feed`: `glimpse | foreground | away | reopened`
- `presence`: `accumulating | accepted`
- `kerni`: `peripheral | in_range | line_open | acknowledged`
- `assembly`: `parts_0 | parts_1 | parts_2 | parts_3 | carrying`
- `placement`: `idle | valid | invalid | pending | accepted | failed`
- `invite`: `idle | copying | copied | manual | cancelled | failed`
- `witness`: `unavailable | consent | pending | accepted | cancelled | failed | replay`
- `lens`: `unavailable | eligible | consent | pending | accepted | cancelled | failed`

These names are a UI contract vocabulary, not a requirement to couple scene components to a transport SDK.

## Responsive and Desktop Runtime Contract

- **Primary gameplay viewport:** desktop browser at 1280×720 and above. All required controls, subtitles, `OPEN`, error copy, and consent actions fit without browser-page scrolling.
- **1280×720 layout:** top chrome max 56px; bottom hint max 56px; side panels max 384px / 34vw; consent sheet max 480px centered. Panels avoid the relay's projected screen bounds where possible and never hide the fixed socket during placement.
- **960–1279px:** preserve gameplay if a fine pointer and keyboard are present; collapse nonessential top chrome; feed remains max 360px; panels may use 16px viewport insets.
- **Below 960px or coarse-pointer mobile Web:** companion-only. Do not expose walking, assembly, placement, or signer-as-gameplay controls. Show a dark Palace companion card: `Werkstattgasse is played on desktop.` and, for invite URLs, `This relay invite is ready. Open it on a desktop browser to touch the relay.` The invite URL may be copied. Do not pretend the mobile page completed a touch/pulse.
- **Rendering budget:** mobile-class hardware remains the asset/effects budget even though mobile Web is companion-only. Target 30 FPS; no new per-frame DOM loop, high-count particles, shadow-casting relay lights, or uninstanced prop multiplication. The relay uses at most one non-shadow point light and static emissive geometry; pulse is one bounded effect.
- Browser zoom to 200% must retain all actions through panel reflow/scroll inside the focused modal, not horizontal page scrolling.

## Accessibility Contract

### Keyboard and focus

- Every action is reachable by `Tab` and activatable by `Enter`/`Space`; world proximity actions additionally use `E`.
- `E` is ignored while an input/button/modal action owns focus; it never signs, places, or dismisses through a focused consent sheet.
- Visible focus uses the existing 2px `#f3d27a` outline with 3px offset on every interactive control, not buttons only.
- Opening feed/dialog/invite/consent moves focus to the heading or first safe control; modal focus is contained; closing returns focus to the invoking world control.
- `Escape` closes the topmost non-pending overlay. During signing pending, `Escape` invokes the same fail-closed cancellation path as `Cancel signing`.
- Pointer-lock/movement keys are released whenever focus enters a DOM control.

### Status, sound, and vision

- Status uses text plus icon/shape: open circle + `OPEN`; check notch + `VALID`; crossed seam + error text; three-notch pending indicator + pending text.
- `aria-live="polite"` announces placement acceptance/failure, invite copy result, signed-pulse result, and lens result once. Proximity hints and accumulating presence are not live-announced repeatedly.
- Kerni dialogue, world audio cues, mechanical click, and pulse all have subtitles. Subtitles are 14px minimum, two lines maximum, on a `#120d08` 88% panel.
- Muting never hides information. Reduced lighting/bloom never removes valid fit, relay open, witness, or lens distinction.
- Do not use sound frequency, bloom, cyan/amber, or movement as the only indicator.

### Motion and readability

- Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`; provide an in-experience `Reduced effects` toggle that also disables bloom/particles even when the OS preference is absent.
- No camera shake, forced camera pan, rapid flashing, parallax panel, or more than three flashes in one second.
- Essential body copy meets 4.5:1 contrast and wraps without clipping. Utility labels are short and remain legible at 200% zoom.
- The full under-ten-minute path is operable with keyboard only in the desktop runtime.
